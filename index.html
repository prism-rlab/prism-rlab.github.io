Tales Imbiriba is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Massachusetts Boston. His research focuses on Bayesian inference, online learning, and physics-guided machine learning, with applications in human-centered technologies, remote sensing, and enhanced security technologies.
